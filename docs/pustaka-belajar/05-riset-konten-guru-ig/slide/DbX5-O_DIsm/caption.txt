Guru Pūrṇimā always makes me think about the difference between the two words we use interchangeably. 
A teacher gave me tools. Better alignment, stronger practice, a body that moved with more ease. That mattered, and I am grateful for it.
But the people who actually changed how I see things did not fit that role. 

Some of them I studied under directly. Some of them I only know through what they left behind, teachings that outlasted the person who gave them. Neither relationship asked to be called guru. I just noticed, later, what had actually shifted in me.

I do not think everyone needs a guru. But I think everyone should know the difference between someone who improves your practice and someone who changes how you see yourself. They are not the same thing, and most people never separate the two.

Who has been that for you, whether they knew it or not?
