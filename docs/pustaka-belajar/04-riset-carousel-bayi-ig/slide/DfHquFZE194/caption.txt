❤️ Good parenting isn’t about doing everything for our children or making them figure everything out alone.

It’s about knowing when to step in and when to step back. 🤍

Let them try, make mistakes, feel their emotions, and learn—with the reassurance that you’re always there when they need you. 🌱

The goal isn’t to raise children who never need us, but children who feel secure enough to become independent. 🫶🏻
