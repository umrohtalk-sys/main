Sometimes, “How was school?” is just too easy to answer with a simple “fine.” ❤️‍🩹

Kids don’t always know how to explain what happened during their day, especially when something is embarrassing, confusing, upsetting, or even surprisingly wonderful. Sometimes they need a question that gives them a little more room to talk. 🥹💭

Try asking things like:
✨ What made you laugh today?
🍎 Who did you sit with at lunch?
🏃‍♂️ What was the hardest part of today?
💡 Did anything surprise you?

A simple question can open the door to a much bigger conversation. One answer can lead to stories about friendships, worries, funny moments, school struggles, confidence, kindness, and things they may never have thought to mention on their own. 🫶

And sometimes, your child doesn’t need you to immediately fix the problem. They just need to know that you’re interested, that you’re listening, and that their little everyday experiences matter to you. ❤️

Those small conversations in the car, at dinner, before bed, or while walking home can become the moments they remember most. 🌱👨‍👩‍👦

Ask less for a report. Ask more for a story. Listen without rushing to solve. ❤️

Because behind a one-word “fine” might be a whole story waiting to come out. 🥰✨
